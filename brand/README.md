## Permitted use

You may use the official Restage assets, without modification, to accurately
refer to Restage, including:

- In articles, reviews, presentations, and documentation about Restage.
- In integration directories or compatibility listings.
- When linking to the Restage website or official repository.

You may resize an asset proportionally and choose the appropriate light- or
dark-background variant. Do not otherwise alter the asset.

## Choosing an asset

- **Mark:** the standalone Restage symbol, for compact or square placements.
- **Wordmark:** the Restage name without the standalone mark.
- **Lockup:** the mark and wordmark together.
- **Light:** intended for light backgrounds.
- **Dark:** intended for dark backgrounds.
- **Static:** preferred for documentation, navigation, and other persistent UI.
- **Animated:** intended for prominent brand moments where motion is
  appropriate.

Use the supplied files rather than recreating the artwork.
