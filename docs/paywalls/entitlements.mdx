title: Entitlements
description: Keep entitlement checks and purchase verification in your app’s billing integration.
---

An entitlement is a feature gate, like `pro`, that a purchase unlocks and your app checks. Several products, such as monthly and annual, can grant the same entitlement.

## Where entitlements live

Keep entitlement checks in your store library, billing service or own server. Gate features on that source. If a surface needs to show something, like whether the user already has Pro, pass it as [host data](/authoring/host-data).

A surface can't unlock anything by itself. It sends events, and your app decides what they mean.

## Restore

Give the paywall a restore button that sends an event:

```dart
TextButton(
  onPressed: paywallEvent('restore_purchases'),
  child: const Text('Restore purchases'),
)
```

Handle `restore_purchases` in `onEvent` and call your store library's restore. That's how a user who reinstalls the app or switches devices gets their subscription back. Offer it wherever the stores expect it.

`restore` and `restage.restore` are reserved. The build stops with an error if a surface uses them.

## Adapter callbacks

The [RevenueCat adapter](/paywalls/revenuecat) hands customer information to `onCustomerInfo`. Run your RevenueCat entitlement checks there. Its restore button uses `revenuecat.restore`.

The [native store bridge](/paywalls/native-stores) calls your app's `verifyPurchase` and `deliverPurchase`. Record each delivery by its verified transaction id, and a purchase the store sends again can't grant consumable content twice. Its restore button uses `in_app_purchase.restore`.

A restore request doesn't return a complete entitlement snapshot. Your billing service stays the source for access decisions.

See [Products and purchases](/paywalls/products-and-purchases) for purchase handling.
