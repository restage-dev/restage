---
title: Entitlements
description: Keep entitlement checks and purchase verification in your app’s billing integration.
---

An entitlement is a feature gate, such as `pro`, that a purchase unlocks and your app
checks. Several products (monthly, annual) can grant the same entitlement.

## Where entitlements live

Keep entitlement checks in your store library, billing service, or own server. Gate
features on that source, and pass whatever a surface needs to show, such as whether
the user already has Pro, as [host data](/authoring/host-data).

A surface can't unlock anything by itself. It sends events, and your app decides what
they mean.

## Restore

Give the paywall a restore button that sends an event:

```dart
TextButton(
  onPressed: paywallEvent('restore_purchases'),
  child: const Text('Restore purchases'),
)
```

Handle `restore_purchases` in `onEvent` and call your store library's restore. Restore
is how a user who reinstalls the app or switches devices gets a subscription back, so
offer it wherever the stores expect it.

The names `restore` and `restage.restore` are reserved, and the build stops with an
error if a surface uses them.

## Adapter callbacks

The [RevenueCat adapter](/paywalls/revenuecat) returns customer information through
`onCustomerInfo`; apply your RevenueCat entitlement checks there. Its restore
button uses `revenuecat.restore`.

The [native store bridge](/paywalls/native-stores) uses your app's `verifyPurchase`
and `deliverPurchase` callbacks. Record each delivery by its verified transaction id,
so a purchase the store sends again can't grant consumable content twice. Its restore button uses
`in_app_purchase.restore`. A restore request does not return a complete entitlement
snapshot; your billing service remains the source of access decisions.

See [Products and purchases](/paywalls/products-and-purchases) for purchase handling.
