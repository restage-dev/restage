Restage uses one license for what runs in your app and your tools, another for the
build-time toolchain, and keeps the hosted service proprietary. Each package includes
its own `LICENSE` file, and that file is what applies. This page is a summary.

## BSD-3-Clause

Everything that runs in your app, and the tools you run against Restage, are
BSD-3-Clause:

- the SDK, [`restage`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage);
- the widget catalogs, [`restage_core`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_core),
  [`restage_material`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_material), and
  [`restage_cupertino`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_cupertino);
- the shared formats and schemas, [`restage_shared`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_shared),
  [`rfw_catalog_schema`](https://pub.dev/packages/rfw_catalog_schema), and
  [`restage_measurement_schema`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_measurement_schema);
- the A2UI check, [`restage_a2ui`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_a2ui);
- the `restage` CLI and its TUI, the MCP server, `restage_revenuecat`, `restage_store_bridge`, and the examples.

The CLI, MCP server, `restage_revenuecat`, and `restage_store_bridge` aren't on pub.dev yet. Their source is
in the [public repository](https://github.com/restage-dev/restage).

BSD-3-Clause allows use, modification, redistribution, and commercial use, as long as
you keep the license's copyright notice and disclaimer. Flutter uses the same license.

## FSL-1.1-ALv2

The build-time toolchain, [`restage_codegen`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_codegen)
and [`rfw_catalog_compiler`](https://pub.dev/packages/rfw_catalog_compiler), uses the
Functional Source License, version 1.1, with Apache 2.0 as the future license.

These packages run when you build. They don't ship in your app. The source is available.
Using them to build your apps is free, including commercial apps and use inside your own
company. The license restricts only competing commercial use, as its text defines it.
Each release converts to Apache-2.0 two years after it ships.

## Restage Cloud

Restage Cloud, the hosted delivery service, dashboard, and backend, is
proprietary. You don't need it to author surfaces or render their compiled-widget
fallbacks. Bundled artifacts also work offline. You can
also serve the generated artifacts from your own server or CDN through the SDK's
resolver interface.

The license of a published package release stays with that release. This page doesn't
promise anything about packages that haven't been released.
