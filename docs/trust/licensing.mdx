What runs in your app and the tools you run against Restage are BSD-3-Clause. The build-time toolchain is FSL-1.1-ALv2, and the hosted service is proprietary. Each package includes its own `LICENSE` file, and that file is what applies. This page is only a summary.

## BSD-3-Clause

BSD-3-Clause covers everything that runs in your app and the tools you run against
Restage:

- the SDK, [`restage`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage);
- the widget catalogs, [`restage_core`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_core),
  [`restage_material`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_material), and
  [`restage_cupertino`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_cupertino);
- the shared formats and schemas, [`restage_shared`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_shared),
  [`rfw_catalog_schema`](https://pub.dev/packages/rfw_catalog_schema), and
  [`restage_measurement_schema`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_measurement_schema);
- the A2UI check, [`restage_a2ui`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_a2ui);
- the `restage` CLI and its TUI, the MCP server, `restage_revenuecat`, `restage_store_bridge`, and the examples.

The CLI, MCP server, `restage_revenuecat` and `restage_store_bridge` aren't on pub.dev
yet. Their source is in the [public repository](https://github.com/restage-dev/restage).

BSD-3-Clause lets you use, modify and redistribute the code, including commercially. You have to keep the license's copyright notice and disclaimer, and Flutter uses the same license.

## FSL-1.1-ALv2

The build-time toolchain is [`restage_codegen`](https://pub.dev/packages/restage_codegen)
and [`rfw_catalog_compiler`](https://pub.dev/packages/rfw_catalog_compiler). Both use the
Functional Source License, version 1.1, with Apache 2.0 as the future license.

These packages run when you build. They don't ship in your app, and their source is
available. Using them to build your apps is free. That includes commercial apps and use
inside your own company. The license restricts only competing commercial use, as its text
defines it. Each release converts to Apache-2.0 two years after it ships.

## Restage Cloud

Restage Cloud is the hosted delivery service, dashboard and backend. It's proprietary.
You don't need it to author surfaces or to show your compiled widgets as their fallbacks.
Bundled artifacts also work offline. You can serve the generated artifacts from your own
server or CDN through the SDK's resolver interface.

A published package release keeps the license it shipped with. This page doesn't promise
anything about packages that haven't been released.
